What We Believe

Core Values

The Bible is Infallible

We believe the Bible is the infallible Word of God and that men were inspired as they wrote the scriptures. (2 Timothy 3:16, 2 Peter 1:20-21)

The Godhead

We believe in the Godhead — the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost are God and are one (meaning united) but distinctive in persons. (1 John 5:7, Matthew 3:16-17)

Death, Burial & Resurrection

We believe in the Death, Burial, and Resurrection of Jesus Christ. (Romans 10:9)

Baptism in the Holy Ghost

We believe that we must be baptized in water and in the Holy Ghost. (Acts 2:38)

Divine Healing & Miracles

We believe in Divine Healing, Miracles, and Deliverance. (Mark 16:17-18)

Heaven, Hell & The Return of Christ

We believe there is a heaven and a hell, and that Jesus Christ is coming back for a church without blemish. (Revelations 20:11-15, 1 Corinthians 15:24-28)
